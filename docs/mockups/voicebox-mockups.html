Principles
Clarity over decorationOne primary action per screen. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and spacing — not borders and boxes.
Status is always visibleEvery complaint shows where it is, who has it, and what happens next. No one has to ask twice.
Security you can feel, not fightAccess rules are enforced on the server; the interface simply never shows what a role cannot reach.
Forgiving by defaultInline validation, clear error messages that never leak detail, and confirmation only for irreversible steps.
Accessible and responsiveAA contrast, visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, and a layout that adapts from phone to desktop.
